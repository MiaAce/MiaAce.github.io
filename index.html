    FAQ

    Questions

    Does it work in Russia?

    Yes, that is who it is built for. The server is in Germany, and the unblock-without-server mode helps Discord and YouTube even when a VPN connection is hard to make.

    When do my days start?

    When you first enter the key in the app. You can buy now and start later.

    Can I use one key on two computers?

    No. A key locks to the first PC that activates it. If you change your PC, message support and we will move it for you.

    How do I pay?

    In our Telegram bot. Crypto (USDT, TON, BTC and more) through @CryptoBot gives you the key instantly. You can also pay the seller directly and get the key as soon as the payment is confirmed.

    Windows warns about the app. Is it safe?

    Ace-VPN is new and not yet code-signed, so Windows SmartScreen may show a warning. Click More info, then Run anyway. Only download it from our bot.

    Which devices are supported?

    Windows 10 and Windows 11, 64-bit.
